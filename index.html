Supported devices

TMWI (The modded web interface) targets Realtek RTL9607C based GPON ONUs. These are the supported devices:

DeviceChipsetStatusNotes
Prolink PGN6401V RTL9607Cv2 view on hack-gpon Tested Primary target.
YOTC M2-2050-G40 RTL9607C/Cv2 view on hack-gpon Tested The one that started it all.
Disclaimer Flashing firmware can soft-brick your GPON ONU if done improperly,it is possible to recover it using UART, we are not liable if you accidentally break things.

Update

Once installed, updates come from inside the interface.

Software Update
  • Open TMWI → Software Update and press Check for updates, or turn on automatic updates (checked every 6 hours).
  • Updates are downloaded from GitHub, verified by SHA-256 and applied atomically.
  • Your users, vouchers, income and portal customisations are kept.
  • If the device is unhealthy afterwards it rolls back automatically. You can also roll back by hand right after an update.
